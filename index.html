02 / Engineering approach

Make the guarantees testable.

In clinical software, reliability and privacy should be properties the system proves—not assumptions people have to remember.

A

Reproducible results

Versioned engine and guideline identifiers, so a stored result stays reproducible after the model moves on.

B

Privacy that is verified

A public build that must never store or transmit input is verified by test, not by convention.
